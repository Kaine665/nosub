Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

This policy explains how Wuxi Gongqian Technology Co., Ltd. handles information when you use the NoSub extension, website, and subscription services.

1. Extension data

  • YouTube subtitles: read from the active YouTube page to provide listening controls. Subtitle data stays in your browser except when you explicitly use translation or lookup features.
  • Preferences: subtitle, language, and interface settings are stored locally using Chrome storage and are removed when you uninstall the extension.
  • Dictionary and examples: a selected word may be sent to Dictionary API or Tatoeba to retrieve definitions or examples.
  • Translation: text you ask to translate may be sent to Google Translate. We do not send your account identity or browsing history with these requests.

2. Website, account, and subscription data

Our pricing website records limited first-party analytics such as page views, an anonymous browser identifier, referring domain, campaign parameters, page path, and time. We use this information to understand conversion and improve NoSub. We do not include your full browsing history or payment details in these analytics events.

When you buy or manage NoSub Pro, Paddle collects payment and checkout information under its own privacy notice. Paddle may provide us with your email address, Paddle customer and subscription identifiers, plan, subscription status, and transaction status so we can activate access, manage your subscription, prevent fraud, and provide support. We do not receive or store your full card number.

3. What we do not do

NoSub does not sell personal data, run behavioral advertising, collect your general browsing history, or use fingerprinting. We do not collect extension usage analytics in the current version.

4. Retention and security

Local extension preferences remain on your device. Subscription records are retained while your account or subscription is active and afterward as reasonably required for support, fraud prevention, accounting, dispute resolution, and legal compliance. We use access controls and encrypted connections designed to protect these records.

5. Your choices and rights

You can uninstall the extension to remove local settings and cancel a subscription through Paddle’s customer portal. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of personal data. Contact us to make a request. Paddle checkout requests should also be directed to Paddle Buyer Support.
